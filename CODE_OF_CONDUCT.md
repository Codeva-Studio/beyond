# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in BEYOND a respectful and constructive experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Give clear, constructive feedback.
- Focus criticism on ideas and work, not people.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and sharing private information without consent
are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the project maintainers through a verified contact method
published by the repository. Do not post sensitive details in a public Issue.
